# Free AI Used-Car Buying Kit 🚗

**A practical, copy-and-paste checklist for buying a used car without guessing.** Free to use. No registration, payment, or email required.

## 1) Before you visit: build the inspection plan

Copy this prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or another assistant:

> I'm considering a used [YEAR] [MAKE] [MODEL] [TRIM/ENGINE] with [MILEAGE] miles, asking [PRICE] in [STATE/COUNTRY]. Give me a one-page buyer's checklist: (1) documents and title checks, (2) questions for the seller, (3) warning lights and safety checks, (4) road-test observations, (5) what a professional mechanic should inspect, and (6) deal-breakers. Mark model-specific reliability claims UNVERIFIED unless you cite an authoritative source. Do not invent a vehicle history, measurements, pricing data or inspection results.

## 2) Compare two cars without invented facts

> Compare Car A [YEAR/MAKE/MODEL/MILES/PRICE] and Car B [YEAR/MAKE/MODEL/MILES/PRICE] for my needs [COMMUTE/BUDGET/WEATHER/FAMILY]. Make a table of ownership costs, likely maintenance categories, safety considerations, unknown history, and test-drive priorities. Do not pretend to know current local asking prices or actual service records. List the five missing facts that would most change the decision.

## 3) Translate seller claims into things to verify

> A seller says: [COPY THE ACTUAL LISTING TEXT]. Separate (A) testable facts, (B) marketing language and (C) missing evidence. Draft six polite questions requesting service records, title/VIN history, repairs, diagnostic reports, and inspection permission. Flag statements that need physical inspection. Do not accuse the seller of dishonesty without evidence.

## 4) Inspect an estimate instead of guessing

> Here is a repair estimate with names and private details removed: [PASTE ESTIMATE]. Explain each labor operation in plain English, potential related parts, questions about diagnosis and warranty, and what needs to be inspected before approving repair. Do not invent labor times, pricing, parts compatibility or repair necessity.

## 5) Use this 60-second buyer decision card

| Item | Status |
| --- | --- |
| VIN matches documents and vehicle | Confirmed / Not checked |
| Title, liens and applicable history checked | Confirmed / Not checked |
| Open recalls checked using official sources | Confirmed / Not checked |
| Cold start + full road test completed | Confirmed / Not checked |
| Independent pre-purchase inspection offered | Confirmed / Declined / Not checked |
| Repair budget and walk-away price decided | Yes / No |

In the U.S., check recalls with [NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls). A recall lookup does not replace a vehicle-history review or professional inspection.

**Important:** An AI assistant cannot physically inspect a vehicle. Its answers can be wrong. Confirm actual defects, service history, recall status and safety with primary records and a qualified mechanic.

## 6) Compare the real drive-away price

Write down the **agreed vehicle price, taxes and registration, and mandatory dealer fees or add-ons** for each offer. Ask for the complete quote in writing so you can see which charges are already included.

| Hypothetical offers | Offer A | Offer B |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Vehicle price | $13,950 | $14,100 |
| Taxes and registration | $800 | $800 |
| Dealer fees and required add-ons | $650 | $150 |
| **Out-the-door total** | **$15,400** | **$15,050** |

Offer B is $350 lower before repairs, insurance, fuel and financing. Use the written numbers and keep any unknown charge blank until the seller confirms it.

> Please send me an itemized out-the-door quote, including taxes, registration, dealer fees and any required add-ons. Which items are optional? Please show any trade-in or financing offer separately.
